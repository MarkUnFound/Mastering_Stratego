### Phase 2: NEW Technical Advancements to Highlight
The core of your presentation must focus on these recent architectural shifts found in the codebase and LaTeX documents:

**1. Architectural Evolution (From Vanilla DQN to MARQ)**
- **Old:** Generic "DQN".
- **New:** A 21.7M parameter **Rainbow DQN** incorporating C51 Distributional RL (51 atoms), Dueling Heads, Multi-step returns (N=5), and Noisy Networks for exploration. 
- **Vision Backbone:** A 6-block ResNet (64-channel) combined with a 4-head Spatial Self-Attention layer, achieving a 27x27 receptive field without the computational overhead of deeper CNNs.

**2. Memory & Information Inference (AAREN Integration)**
- **Old:** Explicit Probabilistic Belief States (PBS) estimating $O(N^2)$ piece probabilities.
- **New:** Total replacement of PBS with **AAREN (Attention as a Recurrent Neural Network)**. 
- **Detail:** A 3-layer, 64-dimensional AAREN encodes opponent action sequences into *implicit* embeddings. It provides parallel training efficiency (like Transformers) with sequential real-time inference (like RNNs). The 64-dim AAREN tensor is concatenated with the 15-channel board state, passing a 79-channel tensor to the DQN.

**3. Reward System Overhaul (Solving the "Happy Wanderer")**
- **Problem:** The agent previously stagnated by shuffling pieces endlessly to farm dense shaping rewards (material, positional) rather than capturing the flag.
- **Solution:** Implementation of **Potential-Based Reward Shaping (PBRS)**. Additive shaping was replaced by a potential function $\Phi(s)$ evaluating material advantage (40%), flag proximity (35%), penetration (15%), and info gain (10%). This guarantees policy invariance while enforcing progress. Additive strict anti-stall penalties target A->B->A piece oscillation.

**4. Curriculum & League Training (PFSP)**
- **Detail:** A 5-Phase curriculum gates training. Transitions strictly require decisive win-rate thresholds (>55% actual wins). 
- **Opponent Scheduling:** Implements a **Prioritized Fictitious Self-Play (PFSP)** adaptive scheduler. Opponents the agent masters are weighted at a minimum 5% floor, while difficult opponents receive proportionally more matchmaking time.
- **MARL Pipeline:** Agent 2 (the opponent) is an active learning agent, but its weight updates and target updates are strictly gated behind Phase 4 (League Training).

**5. Test-Time Evaluation**
- **Detail:** The GUI utilizes a formal **Expectamax Search**, replacing standard minimax to account for chance nodes (hidden piece identities). This mitigates "hallucination" by weighting the DQN's Q-values against AAREN's rank probability expectations.
